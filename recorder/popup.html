Before we start: please read

What is recorded

  • Which apps and buttons you click
  • How much you type, but never what you type
  • When you copy and paste between apps
  • Error and warning messages the app shows you (e.g. "invoice already exists")
  • Screenshots of the allowed work sites only, with form fields blacked out

What is never recorded

  • The text you type, and passwords
  • Any site that is not on the list below

You can pause at any time (here or with the badge on the page). Results are reported per process, not per person. Raw recordings are deleted after the analysis.
